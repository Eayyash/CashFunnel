Each funnel stage is owned by a different part of the journey. The customer-facing screens only move the needle where the drop is UX- or process-driven; the two largest leaks sit in credit/policy and vetting, while the smallest — but most expensive — leak sits in the e-signature & disbursement screens, where credit has already been spent.

▲ Biggest leak · Initial → Final approval

41,907 → 12,158  −71% · ~29,700 lost

The single largest absolute drop, and it is ops/vetting-addressable: employment verification (GOSI/MOF), Sales Vetting of unlisted companies, manual document collection (CR, bank statement, LOS), and the 2nd SIMAH. Known gaps — customers don’t know what’s pending (doc / salary / portal), no proactive detection, no flag for apps aged >48h. Automating company listing (Watheq) and pending-item clarity attacks this stage directly.

◆ Highest cost-per-drop · Final → Booked

12,158 → 10,455  −14% · ~1,700 lost

Only 14% drop, but every lost application here is fully credit-approved — the most expensive abandonment in the funnel. It lives entirely in the Patch-2 screens (approval call → IBAN → Mada card → Sharia docs → NAFATH → Nafith). Known gaps — IBAN not mandatory in back office, no unified calling system, and customers needing active guidance through e-signing. Target: booking-to-final >90%.
